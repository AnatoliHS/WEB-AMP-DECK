Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The Amp Deck™ is a physical product, and all sales are final. We do not offer refunds or accept returns.

Damaged or misprinted decks

If your Amp Deck™ arrives damaged, or a card is misprinted or missing, we will replace it at no cost to you, including shipping.

To request a replacement, email support@ampvisualmedia.com within 14 days of delivery and include:

  • Your name and the email used at checkout
  • A photo of the damage or misprint

We’ll confirm your request and ship your replacement.

Lost or undelivered orders

If your tracking shows your order as delivered and you haven’t received it, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date. We’ll work with the carrier to resolve it.

The digital field guide

The Amp Deck™ field guide is delivered as a digital PDF. It is not refundable. If your download link or file doesn’t work, email us and we’ll fix it.
